LAB THESIS / 02

A model cannot understand the world from the outside.

It has to encounter consequences: force, uncertainty, repair, other agents, and the irreversible passage of time. The Numi Suite brings human mechanics, neural control, molecular biology, and physical simulation into an Apple-native research environment. Each system keeps its own methods and measured limits.

NUMI SOLVER / DEFORMABLE CONTACT

A bag that carries.
A world that responds.

The completed bag replay follows 1,465 cloth particles and twelve dynamic fruits. Four fruits leave the bag, fall, and land on the support plane, then keep rolling. Two four-second CPU FP64 replays pass at 48 substeps with identical physical state. A finer timestep still fails the knot-angle check; the updated full Metal replay is being qualified. Material calibration and real-time performance remain open.

Recorded simulation media from Numi Solver. The fixed camera shows all 49 exported states. Source and frame fingerprints.

NUMI SOLVER / ELASTIC VOLUMES

Native compression.
Elastic recovery.

A volume with 13 shared nodes and 20 tetrahedra falls onto the plane, compresses from 85.1 to 50.9 mm, and recovers to 77.6 mm. Two native replays match all 101 saved states; a finer timestep differs by at most 60 µm. This is one coarse mesh with authored material and frictionless plane contact. Coupling deformable fruit to the woven bag remains open.

Read the elastic mesh record ↗

The video renders the exact native boundary without smoothing or posed deformation. Source, state, and video fingerprints. Mesh refinement, calibrated fruit properties, and complete contact-work closure remain open.
