LAB THESIS / 02

A model cannot understand the world from the outside.

It has to encounter consequences: force, uncertainty, repair, other agents, and the irreversible passage of time. The Numi Suite brings human mechanics, neural control, molecular biology, and physical simulation into an Apple-native research environment. Each system keeps its own methods and measured limits.

NUMI SOLVER / STANDALONE ELASTIC VOLUME

81,920 tetrahedra.
Impact and recovery.

A volume with 14,993 shared nodes falls onto a frictionless plane on the M4 Pro. Its mass center falls from 85.07 to 47.46 mm, then recovers to 74.68 mm over 0.5 simulated seconds. The native replay matches exactly; the half-timestep run differs by at most 93.11 µm. All 101 captured states in each run pass independent geometry and mass checks. The independently audited numerical energy bounds are 0.254% and 0.122% of initial energy, within the authored 1% budget. A new matched 25 µs run at 10,240 tetrahedra also passes its captured-state audits, but the two resolutions differ by up to 9.34 mm at matched nodes against the 1 mm target. Spatial convergence fails.

Read the native volume result ↗

The video renders all 101 exact exported native boundary states without smoothing or posed deformation. Source, binary, audit and media fingerprints · Matched-resolution audit. This standalone volume has no fruit or bag. Separate bounded CPU studies reproduce a shared yarn/elastic-body contact step and a preventive face-area candidate; neither repairs the full bag scene. Measured material, reciprocal cloth/fruit coupling and complete physical work closure remain open. Earlier 10,240-tetrahedron video.
