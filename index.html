THE REASON WE BUILD / 01

The best animal study is the one we no longer need.

Our ambition is to replace selected animal experiments with validated, human-relevant simulations—one question, one measured use case at a time.

An animal can be a poor proxy for a human patient. A computer model is not automatically a better one. To earn that claim, it must predict a defined outcome on evidence it did not train on, expose uncertainty, and survive independent comparison with the methods it would replace.

That is why Numi Lab spans molecules, cells, neural tissue, biomechanics, and physical worlds. The aim is a causal path from a proposed intervention to a measurable consequence—not a convincing animation or a promise of clinical readiness.

This direction reflects a wider shift toward validated non-animal methods. FDA roadmap ↗ NIH initiative ↗ EU roadmap ↗ These are external research directions, not endorsements or qualifications of Numi Lab.

THE STANDARD / 03

A simulation should earn the right to replace an experiment.

The goal is not to declare that all animal studies are obsolete. It is to retire a specific study when a better-supported method can answer its question.

  1. 01

    Name the decision.

    Define the exact safety, efficacy, or mechanism question and the evidence a replacement must provide.

  2. 02

    Bind it to human evidence.

    Calibrate the relevant molecular, cellular, tissue, or mechanical model to measured human data, with stated assumptions and uncertainty.

  3. 03

    Predict before the answer is known.

    Freeze the model and forecast held-out outcomes. Compare against a declared baseline without tuning after the reveal.

  4. 04

    Validate for that use.

    Independent replication and context-specific acceptance decide whether the model can reduce, refine, or replace the study.

Where Numi stands today

Numi has executable research components and recorded physical results. It has not qualified an animal-study replacement, a treatment decision, or a patient-specific digital twin. That is the frontier this program is built to reach.

THE MAC AS A LABORATORY / 05

A serious scientific question should not require a supercomputer to begin.

Apple silicon puts a CPU, GPU, and shared memory on one machine. Numi uses Swift, C++, Metal, and native FP64 reference paths to build and inspect physical and biological models locally.

Local execution lets a researcher keep inputs, code, assumptions, and results together. It does not make every experiment fast or every model accurate; those are measured separately.

Set up the plugin on an Apple silicon Mac

From a Numi Lab checkout with the runtime prerequisites installed:

./tools/numi doctor
./tools/numi codex install

The installer registers the repository marketplace and enables the Numi Lab plugin. Start a new Codex task after installation. Building the native runtime requires the Xcode/Metal toolchain, CMake 3.28+, Ninja, SQLite3, and LibXml2.

NUMI SOLVER / STANDALONE ELASTIC VOLUME

81,920 tetrahedra.
Impact and recovery.

A volume with 14,993 shared nodes falls onto a frictionless plane on the M4 Pro. Its mass center falls from 85.07 to 47.46 mm, then recovers to 74.68 mm over 0.5 simulated seconds. The native replay matches exactly; the half-timestep run differs by at most 93.11 µm. All 101 captured states in each run pass independent geometry and mass checks. The independently audited numerical energy bounds are 0.254% and 0.122% of initial energy, within the authored 1% budget. A new matched 25 µs run at 10,240 tetrahedra also passes its captured-state audits, but the two resolutions differ by up to 9.34 mm at matched nodes against the 1 mm target. Spatial convergence fails.

Needle held.
