LAB THESIS / 02

A model cannot understand the world from the outside.

It has to encounter consequences: force, uncertainty, repair, other agents, and the irreversible passage of time. The Numi Suite brings human mechanics, neural control, molecular biology, and physical simulation into an Apple-native research environment. Each system keeps its own methods and measured limits.

NUMI SOLVER / ELASTIC VOLUMES

Native compression.
Elastic recovery.

A volume with 2,057 shared nodes and 10,240 tetrahedra falls onto the plane, compresses from 85.1 to 47.8 mm, and recovers to 70.6 mm on the M4 Pro. Two native replays match exactly; all 101 saved states pass independent geometry checks. A finer timestep differs by at most 335 µm. The 1,280-to-10,240-element comparison differs by 15.0 mm against a 1 mm target, so spatial convergence remains open.
